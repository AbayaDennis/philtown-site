Who we are

A developer with a legacy of landmark communities.

Philtown Properties, Inc. is a Makati-based real estate developer. We began with Makiling Hills and Woodlands, moved into upscale subdivisions with Tagaytay Heights, and entered the high-end condominium market with One McKinley Place and Fairways Tower in Bonifacio Global City.

Mission

To be a leading real estate developer of self-contained, sustainable communities with the infrastructure, facilities and services needed for balanced living.

Vision

To be a leading provider of corporate real estate services.
